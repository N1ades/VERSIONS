Nyades - Stocking Pull

Open on BOOTH ↗

Grab a stocking or sleeve on a VRChat avatar and pull until the fabric tears. Use Repair in the avatar menu to mend it.

Features

■ Pull together with friends
✔ You and your friends can pull at 2 spots at once by default. You can raise the limit to 8.

■ ASMR-style tearing sounds
✔ Hear the fabric rip as the stockings tear. You can replace the sounds for stretching, release and repair.

■ Hold longer for a bigger tear
✔ Keep the fabric stretched a little longer to create a larger tear.

■ Layer the effect over your existing material
✔ Use it on skinned body or clothing meshes with lilToon or Poiyomi. The fabric layer uses the existing geometry.

■ Widen an existing tear
✔ Tear the same spot again to make the opening larger.

■ Pull blockers
✔ Add a leg band to Pull Blockers so the fabric stretches around it.

■ Stretch and tear appearance
✔ Adjust how far the stretch extends from your fingertips, how sheer the fabric becomes and how it catches the light. Customize the length and width of each tear, its edges, frayed threads and how nearby tears join together.

■ Synced tears
✔ Other players see the same tears you do. The tears stay in place when you let go.

Requirements

• VRChat Avatars SDK 3.10.6 or newer • Modular Avatar • Use either Poiyomi 10+ or lilToon 2.4.1+ for the clothing material. • Optional: Gesture Manager for testing in Unity

Setup and use

Install

1. Import the Stocking Pull .unitypackage into your avatar project.

Add Stocking Pull

2. In the Hierarchy, right-click the garment object with the Skinned Mesh Renderer and choose Nyades → Stocking Pull → Add Component.

Choose the meshes

3. Under Meshes, enable the fabric materials. Paint a mask under Tear Protection for areas that should stay intact.
If the tights are painted on the body, prepare a separate tights material with a texture and opacity mask that fit the body UVs. Add the component to the body mesh and enable only the tights slot under Meshes. Expand the material row to adjust Shell Offset (mm).

Adjust the stretch and tears

4. Add overlying clothes to Pull Blockers. Turn on Preview Mask to check coverage.
5. Under Stocking Pull → Grab regions, select the body areas the fabric covers. Turn on Preview Contact and adjust Contact margin until the capsules cover the fabric.
6. Click Test in Play Mode. In the Game view, hold the left mouse button and drag the fabric. Click Save to Component before exiting Play Mode.

Compatibility and limitations

• In standalone VRChat on Meta Quest (Android), players cannot see the Stocking Pull shader effect: the fabric stretching or tearing. • Other players can grab your outfit according to your VRChat interaction settings. • Enable Self Pull to grab it yourself. • Of the 12 grab regions, enable only those covered by the fabric. • Use Performance → Measure to compare your avatar’s PC performance ratings with and without the effect.

For the purchaser’s own VRChat avatars. Redistribution, sharing and resale of the tool, installed files, unitypackage or DLLs are prohibited. Third-party avatar, clothing and shader terms also apply.

Baking an avatar for distribution

To distribute an avatar with Stocking Pull installed, open Bake (Optional) and select Lock. Include the avatar prefab, the baked assets and the Lock Support DLL they require. Keep the authoring package and the backup used to unlock the setup in your own project.
