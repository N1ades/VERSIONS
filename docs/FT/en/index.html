▍What is Nyades FT Your facial expressions in VRChat, with just a webcam. Nyades FT reads your face from a webcam connected to your Windows PC and sends expression parameters directly to VRChat over OSC. It follows VRCFT parameter conventions, so it works right away with avatars already set up for face tracking. VRCFaceTracking does not need to run. Platforms: Windows 10 / 11 (x64), Linux β (x86_64 AppImage, requires WebKitGTK 4.1) ▍Free trial and full version ・Free trial: sends face tracking for 10 minutes per launch. Try it on your setup first. ・Full version: no time limit, with interface color customization. ▍Features ✔ Tracks eye gaze and tongue as well as facial expressions ✔ GPU acceleration on Windows (choose Auto, CPU or GPU for camera, face and tongue processing) ✔ Calibration for perfect expression capture with any face. Automatic and manual modes, multiple capture modes per parameter, smoothing and symmetry, plus an advanced node-based Expression Mapping editor for power users. ✔ Native Face Tracking support for VRChat Open Beta ✔ Video is processed on your PC and never uploaded ✔ Interface color customization (full version: 6 presets, color picker). As a small extra, cat ears can be shown in the preview. Both only change how Nyades FT looks and do not affect your VRChat avatar.

Getting started

▍What you need ・A webcam ・An avatar that supports VRCFT (Unified Expressions) or VRChat Native Face Tracking (Nyades FT does not set up your avatar) ▍First steps ① Download the ZIP and extract it. ② Run the extracted exe. ③ Select your camera and keep your face in view. ④ Enable OSC in VRChat and select a compatible avatar. ⑤ Look straight ahead, relax your face and click "Capture neutral". Then use "Center gaze" to adjust your gaze. Fine-tune expression parameters manually if needed. ※ Updates are installed from inside the app (gear icon → Updates). ▍Use with VRChat's upper-body camera tracking To also move your head, upper body and hands, set up one of these. The virtual camera (Option 1) is recommended: ✓ VRChat's own face detection doesn't add expressions on top of Nyades FT's face tracking ✓ Your face is covered by a mask, so VRChat never sees it ✓ No Windows settings to change ▶ Option 1 — Virtual camera (recommended, experimental) 　① Nyades FT 　　└ Select your physical webcam as the input camera 　　└ Settings → "Virtual camera (OBS, experimental)" → "Install & enable" 　② VRChat 　　└ Settings → Tracking & IK → select "OBS Virtual Camera" as the camera 　　└ Turn on "Enable Selfie Expression" ▶ Option 2 — Share the camera 　Use this if the virtual camera doesn't work. 　① Windows Settings 　　└ Bluetooth & devices → Cameras → your camera 　　└ Advanced camera options → Edit 　　└ Turn on "Allow multiple apps to use camera at the same time" → Apply 　② VRChat 　　└ Main Menu → Settings → Tracking & IK 　　└ Turn on "Enable Selfie Expression" → For hand tracking, turn on "Selfie Expression Also Moves Hands" with either option. ※ If the camera is unavailable, check Windows camera permissions and whether another app is using it.
