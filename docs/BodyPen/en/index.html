BodyPen — Finger painting for VRChat avatars

Open on BOOTH ↗

Paint directly on your avatar with your finger. BodyPen adds surface painting to your VRChat avatar’s body and clothing. Touch the palette near your head to choose a color, draw with your index finger, then erase part of the drawing or clear it from the menu. Windows PC / PC VR. Requires a compatible Material Contacts client and SDK. The tested setup uses VRChat Open Beta; check the requirements before purchase. First day sale: ¥1,200 until November 1 (JST). Regular price ¥2,000 afterwards.

Features

■ Drawing ✔ Draw with your finger on your own avatar and on other people's avatars that have BodyPen. ✔ Others don't need BodyPen to see your drawings or to draw on you. ✔ Draw together: up to 8 touches at once. ■ Control ✔ Decide who can draw and who can use the palette, for yourself and for others. VRChat Contact permissions also apply. ✔ Pick the meshes that can be drawn on (up to 16) and mask the parts you want to protect. ✔ Erase with the eraser, or clear everything from the menu. If you hide the palette, you can still pick colors and draw from the menu. ■ Paint ✔ Change the colors, brush size and how the paint looks. ✔ Lifetime: the paint fades away after a while. ✔ Use it in your own shaders: add it to any material that supports textures. ✔ Spray Droplets: droplets run down while the line stays. ✔ Physical Gravity Flow: the paint itself runs down the surface. ■ Experimental ✔ Per-player color wheel: every painter gets their own color, brightness, brush size and eraser. Keep Lifetime and Physical Gravity Flow off for this. ✔ Adjust performance: use high quality on VeryPoor avatars, or the lightest settings to stay within Good. ■ Setup ✔ Easy setup: your original meshes and materials stay as they are, and NDMF and Modular Avatar set everything up when you build. ✔ The interface is available in English, 日本語, 한국어 and Русский.

Requirements

• Windows PC / PC VR; Built-in Render Pipeline. Quest / Android is unsupported. • Unity 2022.3.22f1. • VRChat Base and Avatars SDK with Material Contacts, Modular Avatar and NDMF. • A compatible VRChat client. The tested configuration uses Open Beta. • Target meshes with UV0 and triangle submeshes. MeshRenderer and SkinnedMeshRenderer are supported. Tested: SDK 3.10.6-beta.2 / Modular Avatar 1.19.0-alpha.2 / NDMF 1.15.0-beta.2. These are the development environment versions, not a promise of support for older stable versions. If Material Contacts is missing, enable prerelease packages and update both Base and Avatars.

Setup and use

1. Extract the downloaded ZIP and import the unitypackage into your avatar project. 2. Install VRChat SDK 3.10.6 or newer (beta at the time of recording). Gesture Manager is optional. 3. Right-click your avatar in the “Hierarchy” and choose “BodyPen” from the menu. (Alternatively: Tools → Nyades → BodyPen → Add to selected avatar.) 4. Select the “Palette” object and move the palette to your preferred body part. 5. Choose which bone the palette follows. The default is “Head”. 6. Select the “BodyPen” prefab and open “Meshes”. 7. Add the meshes you want to draw on. Start with the face mesh. 8. You can turn off drawing on parts of a mesh. For example, “body_options” is used for facial effects; turn it off so it cannot be drawn on. 9. To protect the eyes, enable “Mask”, choose “Black” and click the areas where you don’t want paint. Those areas turn red. Click “Edit” to leave mask editing. 10. Add the remaining meshes: body, clothing and hair (up to 16). 11. Under “Brush”, click “Test in Play Mode”, draw on the selected meshes and adjust the brush settings. 12. Adjust the colors under “Color palette”. 13. To keep changes made in Play Mode, enable “Save values to component” under “Unity Play Mode”. 14. Under “Paint Material”, change how the paint looks, how long it stays, and its texture. 15. You’re ready! Build and upload your avatar and start drawing. Touch the palette with an index finger to select a color or eraser, then touch a configured mesh. Use the menu for drawing permissions, Clear and Hide palette. To interact with others, also enable the relevant VRChat Contact permissions.

Compatibility and limitations

• Tool and control parameters sync, but each client accumulates its own ink history. Late joiners do not receive previous drawings. Pixel-identical image synchronization is not provided. • UV seams, clothing, shaders and camera configurations can affect results; adjust the setup per avatar. • Extra captures and GPU memory are required. Performance selects one shared atlas at 1024 / 2048 / 4096 / 8192. Lifetime and Physical Gravity Flow require an additional ink buffer. • Mirrors are supported. Orthographic and VRChat photo/UI cameras are excluded from paint processing. • Identical avatar copies may interfere through shared shader transport names. • Compatibility with all avatars, shaders, stable clients or older SDKs is not guaranteed.
