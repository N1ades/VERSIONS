BodyPen — Finger painting for VRChat avatars

Open on BOOTH ↗

Paint directly on your avatar with your finger. BodyPen adds surface painting to your VRChat avatar’s body and clothing. Touch the palette near your head to choose a color, draw with your index finger, then erase part of the drawing or clear it from the menu. Windows PC / PC VR. Requires a compatible Material Contacts client and SDK. The tested setup uses VRChat Open Beta; check the requirements before purchase.

Features

• Seven editable colors and an eraser. Colors set in Unity are reflected on the palette. • Separate self/others permissions for drawing and palette interaction. VRChat Contact permissions also apply. • Adjustable opacity, roughness, metallic appearance, lighting, emission, texture and Grain. • Spray droplets keep the original stroke while sparse drops move downward. Physical Gravity Flow moves the paint itself. These are separate, mutually exclusive modes. • Lifetime fading, initial paint textures and drawing pause. • Up to 16 target meshes through NDMF and Modular Avatar. Original mesh and material assets are preserved. • EN / Japanese / Korean / Russian Inspector and avatar menus. • Hide palette removes the physical palette while keeping menu color selection and drawing available.

Requirements

• Windows PC / PC VR; Built-in Render Pipeline. Quest / Android is unsupported. • Unity 2022.3.22f1. • VRChat Base and Avatars SDK with Material Contacts, Modular Avatar and NDMF. • A compatible VRChat client. The tested configuration uses Open Beta. • Target meshes with UV0 and triangle submeshes. MeshRenderer and SkinnedMeshRenderer are supported. Tested: SDK 3.10.6-beta.2 / Modular Avatar 1.19.0-alpha.2 / NDMF 1.15.0-beta.2. These are the development environment versions, not a promise of support for older stable versions. If Material Contacts is missing, enable prerelease packages and update both Base and Avatars.

Setup and use

1. Extract the downloaded ZIP and import the unitypackage into your avatar project. 2. Place BodyPen.prefab under the avatar, or select your avatar and use Tools → Nyades → BodyPen → Add to selected avatar. 3. Add 1–16 target meshes to Meshes in the Inspector. 4. Position the palette relative to the head and adjust the seven colors, brush size and eraser size. The palette follows the Head bone at build time. 5. Build and upload through the normal VRChat SDK workflow; check the result in a compatible client. Touch the palette with an index finger to select a color or eraser, then touch a configured mesh. Use the menu for drawing permissions, Clear and Hide palette. To interact with others, also enable the relevant VRChat Contact permissions.

Compatibility and limitations

• Tool and control parameters sync, but each client accumulates its own ink history. Late joiners do not receive previous drawings. Pixel-identical image synchronization is not provided. • UV seams, clothing, shaders and camera configurations can affect results; adjust the setup per avatar. • Extra captures and GPU memory are required. Performance selects one shared atlas at 1024 / 2048 / 4096 / 8192. Lifetime and Physical Gravity Flow require an additional ink buffer. • Mirrors are supported. Orthographic and VRChat photo/UI cameras are excluded from paint processing. • Identical avatar copies may interfere through shared shader transport names. • Compatibility with all avatars, shaders, stable clients or older SDKs is not guaranteed.
