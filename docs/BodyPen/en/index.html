Features

■ Drawing ✔ Draw with your finger or a compatible contact on your own avatar and on other avatars with BodyPen. ✔ Works for strangers too: other players don't need BodyPen to see drawings or to draw on your avatar. ✔ Draw together: up to 8 simultaneous touch points. ■ Control ✔ Manage permissions: who can draw and which contacts are accepted. ✔ Mask the areas you want to protect. ✔ Easy setup: works through NDMF and Modular Avatar. ✔ Interface in English, 日本語, 한국어 and Русский. ■ Paint ✔ Customize colors, brush size and paint properties. ✔ Lifetime: the paint fades away over time. ✔ Physical Gravity Flow: the paint itself runs down the surface. ✔ Custom shader support: add it to any material that supports textures. ■ Experimental ✔ Per-player color: every painter gets their own color, brightness, brush size and eraser. ✔ Tune performance: high quality on VeryPoor avatars, or maximum optimization for Good.

Setup and use

Install

1. Extract the downloaded ZIP and import the unitypackage into your avatar project. 2. Install VRChat SDK 3.10.6 or newer (beta at the time of release). Gesture Manager or Av3Emulator is recommended.

Add BodyPen and place the palette

3. Right-click your avatar in the “Hierarchy” and choose “BodyPen” from the menu. 4. Select the “Palette” object and move the palette to your preferred body part. 5. Choose which bone the palette follows in the “MA Bone Proxy”. The default is “Head”.

Choose the meshes

6. Select “Meshes” in the “BodyPen” prefab. 7. Drop here the meshes you want to draw on. 8. You can exclude some material slots from drawing. 9. To exclude UV islands or geometry, enable “Mask”, choose “Black” and click the areas where you don’t want paint. Those areas turn red. 10. Click “Edit” to leave mask editing.

Adjust the brush and colors

11. Under “Brush”, click “Test in Play Mode”, test drawing on the avatar in Game view and adjust the brush settings. 12. Adjust the colors under “Color palette”. 13. To keep changes made in Play Mode, enable “Save values to component” under “Unity Play Mode”. 14. Under “Paint Material”, change how the paint looks, how long it stays, and its texture.

Compatibility and limitations

• Each client accumulates its own ink history. Late joiners do not receive previous drawings. • Identical avatar copies may interfere through shared shader transport names. • Compatibility with all avatars, shaders, stable clients or older SDKs is not guaranteed.
